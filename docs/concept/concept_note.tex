\section{Problem, relevance, intended users, and differentiation}
The haemophilia landscape generates signals across clinical trials, scientific publications, pharmaceutical news, regulatory updates, safety communications, congresses, and market-access developments. These sources are fragmented, making it difficult for teams to identify what materially changed and what deserves immediate attention.

\textbf{Relevance to Novo Nordisk:} As a global leader in haemophilia care with key therapies (such as Esperoct, Alprolix, NovoSeven, and investigational denecimig / Mim8) operating alongside evolving non-factor treatments and gene therapies, Novo Nordisk requires rapid, cross-functional intelligence synthesis to maintain clinical leadership, anticipate market shifts, and streamline internal risk review.

\textbf{MetaRadar} addresses this by converting scattered external information into \textbf{source-linked, prioritized, and actionable internal intelligence}. It differs from a standard news dashboard because it does not simply broadcast headlines; it frames every important development around four decision questions:
\band{\textbf{What changed?}\quad $\rightarrow$ \quad\textbf{Why does it matter?}\quad $\rightarrow$ \quad\textbf{Who should review it?}\quad $\rightarrow$ \quad\textbf{What action may be needed?}}
\textbf{Intended users:} Medical Affairs, Regulatory, Safety/Pharmacovigilance, Market Access, and Leadership. The system is decision support for expert review, not a replacement for professional judgment.

\section{Overall concept, key features, architecture, and decision improvement}
MetaRadar is an AI-enabled intelligence radar that ingests relevant external updates, connects related evidence, summarizes developments, assigns priority, and routes signals to the functions most likely to need review.

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{|p{1.95cm}|X|}
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Detect} & Identify relevant haemophilia developments across target channels.\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Classify} & Categorize clinical, publication, news, regulatory, safety, congress, and access signals.\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Normalize} & Identify therapies, companies, diseases, modalities, trial phase/status, and relevant entities.\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Connect} & Associate related evidence across sources to construct integrated signal trees.\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Summarize} & Generate concise, evidence-grounded explanations with source citations.\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Prioritize} & Assess relevance and urgency using explicit criteria and domain thresholds.\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Route} & Direct signals to the appropriate functional reviewers (Medical, Regulatory, Safety, Access).\\
\hline
\textbf{\color{Navy}Calibrate} & Use structured stakeholder feedback to refine routing, scoring, and summary parameters.\\
\hline
\end{tabularx}}

\textbf{Technology Stack \& Architecture:} Next.js 16 App Router (React 19, TailwindCSS) frontend; Python 3.11 FastAPI backend; PostgreSQL 16 with pgvector for relational/vector storage; Local Gemma 2 / Llama 3 for evidence-grounded RAG summarization and classification.

\section{Detection, classification, summarization, priority, and traceability}
\band{\textbf{Ingest}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Detect}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Classify}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Deduplicate}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Link Evidence}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Summarize}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Prioritize}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Route}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Human Review}}
Signals preserve stable source identifiers and provenance. Summaries remain linked to source evidence; the interface distinguishes source-supported facts from AI interpretation and forward-looking hypotheses. Sensitive medical, regulatory, and safety signals require human review.

\section{Stakeholder input and AI calibration}
Stakeholder input improves AI output by giving structured feedback on whether a signal was appropriately summarized, prioritized, and routed. The feedback informs later calibration without giving the AI autonomous decision authority.

\section{Clarifications and stakeholder input requested from Novo Nordisk}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Which haemophilia signal categories should receive the highest baseline priority?
\item Which criteria should most influence urgency: trial phase/status, efficacy, safety, regulatory action, competitive relevance, access impact, or another factor?
\item How should prioritization and routing differ across Medical, Regulatory, Safety, Market Access, and Leadership?
\item What feedback format would be most useful and least intrusive for busy pharma professionals?
\item Which dashboard views and workflows would provide the greatest immediate decision-making value?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Expected outcome}
\RaggedRight
MetaRadar reduces the gap between \textbf{information availability} and \textbf{decision relevance}. It does not replace experts or make decisions; it organizes relevant developments into a traceable workflow so the right function can review the right evidence at the right time.
\band{\textbf{Scattered information}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Connected evidence}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Prioritized intelligence}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Human review}\ $\rightarrow$\ \textbf{Better-informed action}}
